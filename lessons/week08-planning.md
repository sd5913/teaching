2-hour lecture on Agile/Scrum, group-project teamwork, Codex cloud environments, Actions
and delivery, and live OpenCV/MediaPipe demonstrations, followed by the
2-hour PFAD camera tutorial. See `week08-lesson-plan.md` for pacing.

Preserve Week 7 registration and site navigation. The group project is 40% of
the course grade; its rubric is creative idea 25%, code 50%, execution 25%.
Teams can submit their shared repository URL on Canvas from Week 8 and keep
working. Final deadline: 6 December 2026, 23:59 HKT. The Canvas URL must be
added only when it exists.

Use one public repository owned by `sd5913` per group. Students accept their
organization invitation first, choose **Owner: sd5913** when creating a repo,
and grant teammates Write access. The matching organization Project has its own
access settings. Existing personal group repos can be transferred with the
instructor's help, preserving history. Personal-account Projects also exist;
the organization is this course's shared home rather than a GitHub requirement.

Students create their group Project from the organization's
[Group Project 2026 template](https://github.com/orgs/sd5913/projects/1), based on
the iterative-development structure: click **Use this template** and choose
**sd5913** as the owner. It includes one-week iterations, current
and next iteration views, a full board and dated delivery cards. Tutor reviews
cover the class weeks of 5, 12, 19 and 26 November, then 3 December; exact meeting
times follow each group's allocated tutorial slot and are confirmed by the
tutor. The 6 December, 23:59 HKT submission card is separate. Students bring a
usable increment and evidence, record feedback and agree the next goal. This
documents the existing execution criterion without adding a grade component or
Canvas upload. New copies need their own repository links and teammate access.

Agile/Scrum is explicit quiz material. The Week 1 Manifesto recap leads into
iterative/incremental work, Scrum pillars, accountabilities, artifacts and their
commitments, and Sprint events. GitHub Projects/Issues demonstrate shared
planning, ownership and review evidence for the existing 25% Execution
criterion. Distinguish the values, framework and tool. A linked equivalent
progress record remains valid; formal role titles and extra Canvas uploads are
not new requirements. Reserve 20 minutes before Git; retain 30 minutes for
camera concepts and demos within the 120-minute lecture.

without requiring all teams to choose those frameworks or app stores. Their
coding environment, GitHub runner and delivery destination are separate.
Tetrilaunch is the multiplatform delivery case study. Do not put credentials
in slides, source or client bundles.

a pinned version and a separate compatible uv environment. Nine exercise topics
cover the 2025 examples, including a separate classifier and its limitations.
A live camera rehearsal remains required on the presentation machine.
