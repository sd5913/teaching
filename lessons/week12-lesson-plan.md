# Week 12 · Course review and group AMA

Thursday 26 November 2026, Hong Kong. Lecture 120 minutes. The separate [student tutorial](https://github.com/sd5913/pfad/blob/365905e19c307cc245223ed626653d6f4c87438d/week12/README.md) also runs for 120 minutes. Open the candidate, paper and pens before class. Use the exported PDF if slides or network access fail.

| Minute | In the room |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4 | Open with the three company announcements/claims. Keep the news to minutes 1–4 and outside quiz scope. Use the dated Leonida count to ask which observation could establish playability. |
| 4–13 | Ask each pair for a visitor and a first action. Connect Week 1’s README and Week 2’s predicted check to Week 8 peer review. |
| 13–24 | Walk from a saved tide height to a plotted day. Let pairs predict what should happen when switching from January 31 to February. Compare an encoding change with a selected-record change. |
| 24–35 | Reverse a GIF and double a tone frequency. Have groups map their chosen cue to success, then ask what happens if a camera stops seeing a hand. |
| 35–48 | Trace Ball state through two renderers, then a scene through two clients. Ask for a prediction before reconnecting a tab or restarting a server. Which state belongs on disk? |
| 48–60 | Separate Pip’s citation, history and voice; follow an authenticated second visitor or the same Blender object after import. Groups choose the comparison relevant to their medium. |
| 60–70 | Break. Resume at 70. |
| 70–81 | Inspect the short recorded Godot decision and the later deterministic film or still. Ask separately what the checker proves, what the film shows and what only a visitor can tell us. Keep commands in the optional resource. |
| 81–90 | Use two overlapping Leonida repository-check PRs as a review decision. What should be tested on both heads? Which cases and contributors should be credited? Switch to the team’s own delivery candidate. |
| 90–95 | Give groups three minutes to prepare and two to choose an observation and spokesperson. Prompt: “Our visitor tries ___. We saw ___. Should we change ___ or ___?” Collect paper slips or invite spoken questions if offline. |
| 95–115 | Four five-minute AMA rounds. Spend one minute on the question, three on evidence and possible checks, one on a follow-up. Invite groups with different delivery targets. Cluster repeats and carry unanswered questions to tutor support. |
| 115–120 | Each team identifies one next check and an owner. Hear one or two decisions, open the paired tutorial and finish at minute 120. |

Use the [published group-project brief](https://github.com/sd5913/pfad/blob/2026/assignments/group-project.md) for policy. The scheduled 26 November tutor focus is a delivery candidate. The tutorial break is 55–65 and tutor/project support is 100–120. The AMA and optional Godot reference add no submission.
