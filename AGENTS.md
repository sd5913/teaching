# Slide authoring

- Make diagrams fill the slide wherever possible. Use a compact heading and give the diagram the remaining canvas.
- Keep node and arrow labels short and large enough to read from the back of a classroom. Target at least 40 design pixels for primary node and arrow labels on a 1920 × 1080 slide.
- Split a crowded diagram into successive slides, showing one relationship or step at a time. Keep the geometry consistent across steps.
- Move explanations and implementation detail into speaker notes or separate slides; do not shrink the diagram to fit prose beside it.
- Render and inspect the diagrams at presentation size before delivering the deck.
